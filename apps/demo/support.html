A little guidance

Where can I find my invoices?

Open Workspace settings, choose Billing, and select Invoices. Only workspace owners can manage billing.

How do I invite a teammate?

Go to Workspace settings, open Members, and choose Invite member. Your teammate will receive an invitation.

How do I get in touch?

Create a support request using the form on this page. Include a short subject and the details of your question.

A visual assistant can explain these controls and help you submit a request.
